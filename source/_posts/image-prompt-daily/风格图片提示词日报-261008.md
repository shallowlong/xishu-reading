### 1. Kumiko 組子（日本无钉格木 · **一根根细木条靠"切准的角度互相顶死"，不靠钉子不靠胶水** · 精度 0.1mm）
- **来源**：Sawyer Design《Kumiko: The Japanese Lattice Technique That Uses No Nails…》(https://sawyerdesign.io/blog/technique-2026-06-23) ；Japan Woodcraft Association《Kumiko》(https://japanwoodcraftassociation.com/traditions/techniques/kumiko/) ；Wikiwand《Kumiko (woodworking)》(https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Kumiko_(woodworking)) ；风向锚：It's Nice That 2026 趋势报告（handmade texture / craft revival）(https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/forward-thinking-graphic-trends-2026-graphic-design-120126)
- **标签**：微距 / 高对比 / 地域文化
- **一句话特征**：淡色木（榉木/桧木）细条（2–4mm）切成精确斜角榫，一根根拼成几何格，靠木条之间均匀的挤压力自己顶住不散；背后是透光的和纸（shoji 障子），于是"格子"本身变成一块会发光的几何织网。
- **为什么有意思**：反直觉的核心是**"强度来自互锁的压力，不是来自连接件"**——没有一颗钉子、一滴胶，几百年前（奈良时代 710–794，可上溯到飞鸟时代）就玩到 0.1mm 精度，几十种图样各有各的"吉祥寓意"（麻叶 asanoha 辟邪、樱 saku 求丰收、亀甲 tsumiishi-kikko 六边形寓意长寿）；而真正撑住它的是一整块透光的白纸，木头是"阴影骨架"、纸才是"光"，于是同一格子里明暗全在纸的透光率上。
- **通用提示词**：
```
Close-up of Japanese kumiko wooden lattice joinery: thin beech and cypress slats (2–4mm) cut at precise bevel angles and interlocked with no nails and no glue, held purely by even mutual pressure; the classic Asanoha (hemp-leaf) hexagonal star and Sakura (cherry-blossom) five-point motifs; backlit by warm washi shoji paper glowing evenly behind the slats; pale warm wood grain against the bright diffused paper, crisp 0.1mm joints, shallow depth of field, soft window light, minimal, serene
```
- **效果图参考**（agnes-image-2.5-flash，2K / 1:1）：![Kumiko 組子](https://pic1.imgdb.cn/i/034tr8bCNmA1GpZXmXtY1m.webp)
- **分工具适配**：
  - **Midjourney**：
    ```
    /imagine japanese kumiko wooden lattice joinery close-up, thin beech cypress slats interlocked no nails no glue, Asanoha hemp-leaf hexagonal star and sakura cherry-blossom motifs, backlit by warm washi shoji paper, pale warm wood grain, crisp fine joints, soft window light, minimal serene --style raw --s 150 --ar 4:5 --v 6.1
    ```
    参数说明：微距格纹高密度→`--s 150`；`--style raw` 防加戏；`--ar 4:5` 竖版格纹舒服。
  - **即梦 / 可灵 / 通义万相 / Seedream**（中文）：
    - 即梦：`日本组子木格, 榉木细木条, 斜角榫互锁, 无钉无胶, 麻叶六边形星纹, 樱花五瓣纹, 障子和纸背光, 暖色原木, 细腻木纹, 柔和窗光, 微距, 极简禅意`（名词顿号连接、无逗号；`sample_strength` 标「待验证」）
    - 可灵 Kolors：同上文案，`guidance_scale` 给 4–7（格纹密度中档）
    - 通义万相（本条按 V2 写）：主体`日本组子木格障子窗, 斜角榫无钉无胶, 麻叶纹樱花纹, 和纸透光`；`negative_prompt`：`nails, glue, screws, blurry, warped wood, dirty`
    - Seedream（主体+环境，专业词用词源语言，2K）：`a backlit Japanese kumiko shoji panel, interlocking wooden slats no fasteners, Asanoha and Sakura geometric motifs, warm washi paper glow` → 2K
  - **Stable Diffusion（SDXL）**：
    - 正向：`japanese kumiko wooden lattice, thin beech slats, beveled interlocking joints no nails no glue, asanoha hexagonal star motif, backlit shoji paper, warm wood grain, macro, soft light, minimal`
    - 负向：`nails, screws, glue, metal, blurry, lowres, deformed, text, watermark`
    - LoRA 建议：本期未实测；专有名词 `kumiko`/`shoji` 干净，纯靠正向写死；如需可借 `japanese architecture`/`shoji screen` 类近亲件限权 0.2–0.4，不要指望有"组子专属"件。
  - **其他 · Flux.1**：
    ```
    extreme macro of a Japanese kumiko shoji panel, bevel-cut interlocking beech slats held by pressure alone, Asanoha hemp-leaf star and sakura motifs, warm backlit washi paper, pale wood, crisp fine joints, soft window light
    ```
- **避坑**：① 模型极易给"格子"加钉子/螺丝/胶缝（负向必写 no nails/screws/glue）；② "透光的纸"常被画成死白死黑，要 `soft even backlight`，别让纸过曝；③ 格纹会糊成一片，微距只拍 3–5 个格，别铺满；④ 木色别被"暖光"推到橙红失真。
- **可选 · 服装/童装用法**：格木透光这个"骨架+透光面"的观感，天然适合做**童装格纹衬衫/窗棂纹卫衣的面料肌理**——把 shoji 格当面料上的透光织纹来渲染，比硬套"赛博朋克"自然得多。

### 2. Trompe l'oeil Board-Picture（北方欧洲静物派 · "钉在墙上的物件" **画得像真能伸进去摸到**）
- **来源**：Metropolitan Museum of Art《Cubism and the Trompe l'Oeil Tradition》(https://www.metmuseum.org/es/exhibitions/cubism-trompe-loeil%2Fvisiting-guide) ；Christie's《Trompe l'oeil: an essential guide》(https://www.christies.com/stories/trompe-loeil-collecting-guide-acdc663549e84248b94a2191a98dabd6) ；风向锚：It's Nice That 2026 报告
- **标签**：油画 / 高对比
- **一句话特征**：17 世纪北方欧洲（荷兰/佛兰德斯）那一支"板上物"（board-pictures / letter-racks）——把信纸、羽毛、镊子、标本、钉在墙上的图版画得"立体"，物件边缘投下真实的微小阴影，墙是纯色哑光底，物件像真的从画里"戳"出来。
- **为什么有意思**：它的"骗"是**双向的**：画布假装是"墙"，钉子/丝带/绳头也假装是"真的"，观者一瞬间分不清哪层是画、哪层是现实——Pliny 记的那则"宙克西斯画帘子骗过帕尔哈修斯"的寓言就是这个母题；它 19 世纪没落了，1890s 美国镀金年代靠"街头骗术画"复活，1912 年毕加索/布拉克/格利斯又用它喂出立体派拼贴。冷门在于**"画的表面在否认自己是画"**这件事。
- **通用提示词**：
```
Seventeenth-century Northern European trompe-l'oeil board picture: letters, paper documents, a quill, a feather and small specimens painted to look real, hung by tiny nails, tacks and ribbon against a single flat matte wall of muted olive-grey; each object casts a small sharp shadow on the wall, the canvas pretends to be the wall, high realism, chiaroscuro light, oil paint texture, 17th century Dutch still-life, quiet and still
```
- **效果图参考**（agnes-image-2.5-flash，2K / 1:1）：![Trompe l'oeil Board-Picture](https://pic1.imgdb.cn/i/034tr8dmdTZEJgwJ3kUwrN.webp)
- **分工具适配**：
  - **Midjourney**：
    ```
    /imagine 17th century northern european trompe l'oeil board picture, letters paper quill feather and small specimens hung on a flat matte olive-grey wall by tiny nails ribbon and tacks, each object casts a small sharp shadow, the canvas pretends to be the wall, chiaroscuro, dutch still-life, oil paint --style raw --s 100 --ar 3:4 --v 6.1
    ```
    参数说明：识别度全在"极简约束（一面哑光墙+物件小阴影）"→`--s 100` 防模型加戏；`--style raw` 必须。
  - **即梦 / 可灵 / 通义万相 / Seedream**（中文）：
    - 即梦：`17世纪北方欧洲错视静物, 钉在墙上的信纸, 羽毛, 钢笔, 绳头, 纯色哑光橄榄灰墙, 每个物件投下微小硬阴影, 画布假装是墙, 明暗对比, 油画质感, 静物, 安静`
    - 可灵 Kolors：同上文案，`guidance_scale` 给 4–8
    - 通义万相（本条按 V2 写）：主体`钉在纯色哑光墙上的信纸羽毛钢笔, 17世纪错视静物, 微小硬阴影`；`negative_prompt`：`framed picture, wooden frame, colorful wall, modern objects, text`
    - Seedream（主体+环境，专业词用词源语言，2K）：`a 17th-century Dutch trompe-l'oeil board picture, letters a quill and a ribbon pinned on a flat matte olive wall, small sharp shadows, the canvas pretends to be the wall` → 2K
  - **Stable Diffusion（SDXL）**：
    - 正向：`17th century dutch trompe l'oeil still life, letters paper quill feather pinned on a single matte wall, tiny nails and ribbon, each object a small sharp shadow, chiaroscuro, oil paint, the canvas pretends to be the wall`
    - 负向：`framed picture, wooden frame, color wall, modern object, text, watermark, lowres, blurry`
    - LoRA 建议：本期未实测；`trompe l'oeil`/`dutch still life` 有相关近亲件可借（限权 0.3–0.4），但"错视"这层要靠正向写死，别指望 LoRA 自动懂"画布假装是墙"。
  - **其他 · GPT Image / Nano Banana**：直接用上面"通用提示词"即可，这两家对"静物+墙+小阴影"这种极简构图理解最稳。
- **避坑**：① 模型最爱给"物件"套一个画框（负向必写 no framed picture/no wooden frame）；② "墙"会被自动换成花墙/壁纸，要 `single flat matte wall`；③ 物件一多就糊，控制在 4–6 件；④ 光影别太"油画感"的厚涂，要"哑光墙+小硬阴影"的克制。

### 3. Zellige 摩洛哥手工切砖马赛克（**用 mnqach 小锤+钢凿把烧好的釉砖敲成几何碎块，倒扣在地面上靠切缝咬合，不靠胶**）
- **来源**：Design by Curio《What is Moroccan Zellige Tile?》(https://designbycurio.com/learn/moroccan-zellige-tile) ；Morocco Legacy《Exploring Moroccan Zellige Tiles》(http://moroccolegacy.com/blog/zellige) ；Unveiling Morocco《Zellige Tilework: Morocco's Iconic Art of Islamic Geometry》(https://unveilingmorocco.com/2025/11/27/%e2%9c%a8-zellige-tilework-moroccos-iconic-art-of-islamic-geometry/) ；风向锚：It's Nice That 2026 报告（craft / handmade texture）
- **标签**：玻璃 / 分形 / 地域文化
- **一句话特征**：10 世纪起、以菲斯（Fes）为都的伊斯兰几何马赛克——烧好的 bejmat 方砖（900℃ 橄榄渣窑 faytour）釉面是钴蓝/翠绿/藏黄/朱红/白/黑六种矿物色，工匠用 mnqach 小锤敲成几百年不变的老套路：八角星→风筝形/菱形/拉长六边形，满铺不留白（horror vacui），釉面宝石般吸光。
- **为什么有意思**：反直觉点在**"它是倒过来拼、靠切缝咬合、不用胶"**：先在釉砖**反面**画好图样，用锤凿敲成碎块，把碎块**倒扣在地上**按图案严丝合缝咬合（切得准到互相顶死），再覆水泥、翻面、上墙——全程不靠粘贴；且它不是"画图案"而是"满铺不留白"，几何的无限重复是伊斯兰对"神的无限性"的图解。冷在于**"切砖这件事本身"被当成一门可传承七百年、只在菲斯完整存活的技艺**。
- **通用提示词**：
```
Fez-style Moroccan zellige mosaic: hand-cut glazed terracotta tesserae in cobalt blue, emerald green, saffron yellow, ruby red, chalk white and black, assembled upside-down into interlocking eight-pointed stars, kite and elongated-hexagon polygons, dense near-groutless geometry, horror-vacui total coverage, jewel-like mineral glaze depth, light absorbed slowly and returned softly, traditional Fez courtyard fountain or madrasa wainscot
```
- **效果图参考**（agnes-image-2.5-flash，2K / 1:1）：![Zellige 摩洛哥手工切砖马赛克](https://pic1.imgdb.cn/i/034tr9MrC6WAbwaMAayymn.webp)
- **分工具适配**：
  - **Midjourney**：
    ```
    /imagine traditional Fez zellige tile mosaic, hand-cut glazed terracotta tesserae in cobalt blue emerald green saffron yellow ruby red white and black, interlocking eight-pointed stars and kite hexagon polygons, dense near-groutless geometry horror vacui, jewel-like mineral glaze depth, soft light absorbed and returned slowly --style raw --s 180 --ar 1:1 --v 6.1
    ```
    参数说明：满铺几何+矿物釉色高密度→`--s 180`；`--style raw` 防釉色溢出。
  - **即梦 / 可灵 / 通义万相 / Seedream**（中文）：
    - 即梦：`菲斯摩洛哥手工切砖马赛克, 釉面陶土碎砖, 钴蓝翠绿藏黄朱红黑白, 八角星, 风筝形, 六边形, 满铺不留白, 宝石质感釉, 菲斯庭院喷泉, 传统伊斯兰几何, 柔和反光`
    - 可灵 Kolors：同上文案，`guidance_scale` 给 4–7
    - 通义万相（本条按 V2 写）：主体`菲斯风格手工切砖马赛克, 八角星六边形几何, 钴蓝翠绿矿物釉色, 满铺不留白`；`negative_prompt`：`grout lines, modern tile, white grout, text, watermark`
    - Seedream（主体+环境，专业词用词源语言，4K）：`a traditional Fez zellige tile mosaic wall, hand-cut glazed tesserae, eight-pointed stars and interlocking hexagons, cobalt green saffron ruby mineral glaze, dense near-groutless pattern` → 4K
  - **Stable Diffusion（SDXL）**：
    - 正向：`fez moroccan zellige mosaic, hand-cut glazed terracotta tesserae, cobalt blue emerald green saffron yellow ruby red white black, interlocking eight-pointed stars kite and hexagon polygons, dense near-groutless geometry, horror vacui, jewel-like mineral glaze, soft reflected light`
    - 负向：`white grout lines, modern tile, ceramic grid, text, watermark, blurry, lowres`
    - LoRA 建议：本期未实测；`islamic geometry`/`geometric mosaic` 有通用件可借（限权 0.2–0.4，注意别被"伊斯兰艺术/书法"件吃掉、要的是"切砖马赛克"而非"书法"），专名 `zellige` 干净。
  - **其他 · Flux.1**：
    ```
    Fez-style zellige tile mosaic wall, hand-cut glazed tesserae in cobalt, emerald, saffron, ruby, white, black, interlocking eight-pointed stars and hexagons, dense near-groutless horror-vacui geometry, jewel-like mineral glaze depth, soft light
    ```
- **避坑**：① 模型必给你加"白缝"（现代瓷砖既视感），正向要 `near-groutless`、负向必写 `white grout lines/modern tile/ceramic grid`；② 釉色易溢出变荧光（`jewel-like mineral glaze` 拉回来）；③ 满铺几何别铺太满导致"看不清主体"，可以配一个庭院/喷泉/腰墙的环境；④ 六角星会画成乱几何，要 `interlocking` 咬合不是 `random`.
- **可选 · 服装/童装用法**：满铺八角星+风筝形这个"几何不留白"的结构，跟童装的**满版图案卫衣/碎花裙的排布逻辑**其实是同一种"无留白几何"——可以直接借它的密铺排布做满版印花排布，比硬套"民族风"精确。
